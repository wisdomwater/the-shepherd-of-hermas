## Vision 5 — The Shepherd

I was at home praying, seated on the couch, when a man entered.

There was something striking about him. His face was radiant, but he was dressed like a shepherd, with a white skin wrapped around him. A bag hung from his shoulder, and he carried a staff in his hand.

He greeted me, and I greeted him back.

Then he sat down beside me.

“I've been sent by the most holy angel,” he said, “to stay with you for the rest of your life.”

I immediately became suspicious.

I thought he had come to test me.

“Who are you?” I asked. “I know who I've been entrusted to.”

He looked at me.

“You don't recognize me?”

“No.”

“I am the shepherd to whom you were entrusted.”

As he spoke, his appearance changed.

Then I recognized him.

He was the one.

The moment I realized it, fear came over me. I was ashamed of the way I'd spoken to him. I'd answered him foolishly and disrespectfully, and now I didn't know what to do with myself.

But he didn't dwell on it.

“Don't be troubled,” he said. “Strengthen yourself instead in the commandments I'm about to give you.”

Then he explained why he had come.

“I was sent to show you again the things you've already seen, especially the parts that are useful for you. First, write down my commandments and my parables. The other things you will write as I show them to you.”

He wanted those teachings written first for a reason.

“You need to be able to read them readily,” he said, “so that you can keep them.”

So I did what he told me.

I wrote down the commandments and the parables.

And this is what hangs over everything that follows:

If you hear these things and keep them, if you walk in them and do them with a pure heart, you will receive from the Lord everything he has promised you.

But if you hear them and refuse to repent—if you simply continue adding sin to sin—you will receive the opposite.

These are the things the shepherd commanded me to write.

He is the angel of repentance.
