## Mandate 1 — Believe in the One God

“First,” the shepherd said, “believe that God is one.

“He created everything and put everything in its proper order. He brought all things from nothing into existence. He contains and understands everything, while he alone cannot be contained or fully understood.

“Believe in him.

“Fear him.

“And because you fear him, practice self-control.”

He spoke as though everything else would grow from these first things.

“Keep them, and you will strip wickedness from yourself and clothe yourself instead with everything that belongs to righteousness.

“If you keep this commandment, you will live to God.”
