## Vision 3a — The Tower Upon the Waters

I had been fasting often and asking the Lord to show me the revelation he had promised through the old woman. I must have been relentless about it, because one night she appeared to me and said, “You really are determined to know everything, aren't you? Go out into the countryside where you're staying. Around the fifth hour I'll meet you there and show you what you're supposed to see.”

“Where should I go?” I asked.

“Wherever you want.”

I thought of a beautiful, secluded place where we wouldn't be disturbed. Before I could even tell her where it was, she said, “I'll come wherever you choose.”

So the next day I went into the countryside, watching the hours carefully. When the time came, I reached the place I'd chosen and found something already waiting for me: an ivory couch with a linen cushion, covered with fine flax.

But there was no one there.

Something about seeing that couch arranged so deliberately in an empty place terrified me. I began trembling. My hair stood on end, and I felt a shudder go through my whole body.

For a moment, I was simply afraid to be there alone.

Then I remembered the glory of God.

I gathered myself, knelt down, and began confessing my sins again, just as I had before.

While I was still praying, the old woman arrived with six young men—the same ones I'd seen previously. She stood beside me and listened as I prayed and confessed everything to the Lord.

Finally she touched me.

“Hermas, enough. Stop constantly begging forgiveness for your sins. Pray for righteousness too, so that you can take some of it home to your family.”

She took my hand and pulled me to my feet. Then she led me to the couch and turned to the six young men.

“Go and build.”

They left.

Now the old woman and I were alone.

“Sit here,” she told me.

“Lady, shouldn't the elders sit first?”

“Do what I told you. Sit down.”

I went to sit on the right side of the couch, but she immediately stopped me and motioned for me to sit on the left.

I obeyed, but I couldn't help thinking about it. Why wouldn't she let me sit on the right? The more I thought about it, the more disappointed I became.

She noticed.

“Are you sad, Hermas?”

I suppose there was no point denying it.

“The place on the right belongs to others,” she said, “to those who have already pleased God and suffered for the sake of the Name. You still have many things lacking before you can sit with them. But remain in your simplicity, and you will sit with them one day—along with everyone who does what they did and suffers what they suffered.”

“What did they suffer?”

“Listen. Beatings. Imprisonment. Great tribulations. Crosses. Wild beasts. All for the sake of the Name. That is why the right side belongs to them and to everyone who suffers for the Name.”

She looked toward the place where I was sitting.

“The rest sit on the left. But understand this: both receive the same gifts and the same promises. Those on the right simply possess a particular glory because of what they endured.”

She knew exactly what was bothering me.

“You want very badly to sit there with them, Hermas. But you have many shortcomings. You will be purified from them. And everyone who is no longer divided within himself will be purified from the sins committed up to this day.”

Then, to my surprise, she got up as though she intended to leave.

I immediately fell at her feet.

“Lady, please. By the Lord, don't leave yet. You promised to show me a vision.”

Once again she took my hand and raised me. She seated me on the left side of the couch while she sat on the right.

Then she lifted a shining rod.

“Do you see something great?”

I looked around.

“Lady, I don't see anything.”

“Look.”

I looked again.

“Don't you see it there in front of you? A great tower being built upon the waters from shining square stones?”

And suddenly I did.

A great tower was rising out over the water.

The six young men were building it in a perfect square. Countless others were bringing stones to them. Some stones came from deep beneath the water. Others were carried in from the land.

The six builders received each one and placed it where it belonged.

The stones pulled from the deep were astonishing. They needed no cutting or shaping. They were already formed perfectly, and when the builders placed them into the tower, they fit against the other stones so closely that I couldn't see where one ended and another began.

The entire tower looked as though it had been carved from a single enormous stone.

But the stones brought from the land were different.

Some were accepted and placed into the building. Some were rejected. Others were broken apart and thrown far from the tower.

All around it lay many stones that weren't being used at all. Some were covered in mildew. Some were cracked. Some were too short. Others were bright white and perfectly round, beautiful in themselves but completely wrong for the shape of the building.

Then I noticed stones that had been thrown even farther away.

Some rolled toward a road, but they wouldn't stay on it. They wandered off into places where there was no road at all.

Others fell into fire and burned.

Still others landed near the water. They seemed as though they wanted to roll into it, but somehow they couldn't reach it.

I watched everything carefully.

Then the woman started to leave again.

“Lady!”

She turned.

“What good is it for me to see all these things if I don't know what they mean?”

She looked at me with an expression I was beginning to recognize.

“You are an extremely curious man, Hermas. You want to know everything about the tower.”

“Yes, lady. I do. I want to tell my brothers and sisters about it so that when they hear these things they can rejoice and know the Lord in his great glory.”

“Many will hear,” she said. “Some will rejoice. Others will weep. But even those who weep will rejoice if they listen and repent.”

Then she settled herself again.

“Listen, then, to the parables of the tower. I'll explain everything to you. And stop troubling me for more revelations. These revelations are reaching their end.”

She looked straight at me.

“Though you probably won't stop asking. You have no shame.”

I couldn't really argue with that.

“The tower you see being built is me,” she said. “It is the Church, the same one you've already seen. So ask whatever you want about the tower, and I'll reveal it to you so that you may rejoice with the saints.”

“Lady, since you've already considered me worthy to have these things revealed, then reveal everything.”

“Whatever can be revealed to you will be revealed. Only keep your heart fixed on God, and don't doubt what you're seeing.”

I began with the question that seemed most obvious.

“Why is the tower being built on water?”

“I've told you this before,” she said. “But you certainly know how to keep asking. Still, through your questions you discover the truth.”

She pointed toward the tower.

“Your life is saved, and will be saved, through water. But the tower itself is founded upon the word of the Almighty and glorious Name, and it is strengthened by the unseen power of the Master.”

I looked again at the tower rising over the water.

“Lady, this is great. Truly marvelous. But those six young men building it—who are they?”

“They are the holy angels of God, created before all others. The Lord entrusted his whole creation to them, that they might increase it, build it, and oversee it. Through their hands the building of the tower will be completed.”

“And the others bringing them the stones?”

“They too are holy angels of God. But the six are greater than they are. When the tower is finished, they will all rejoice together around it and glorify God because its building has been completed.”

I still had questions.

Of course I did.

“Lady, I want to know what will happen to the stones. I want to understand what they represent.”

She looked at me for a long moment.

“Don't imagine this is being revealed because you are more worthy than everyone else. There are others ahead of you, better than you, to whom these visions might have been given. But this has been revealed through you so that the name of God may be glorified, and especially for those who are divided in their minds, wondering whether these things are really true.”

Her voice became firm.

“Tell them they are true. There is nothing false in them. Everything you have seen is secure, established, and founded firmly.”
