## Vision 4 — The Beast on the Road

Twenty days after the previous vision, I saw another one. This time it was given to me as a sign of the tribulation that was coming.

I was walking alone into the countryside along the Companion Way. The road was easy, and the place I was heading toward wasn't far from the main road.

As I walked, I prayed that the Lord would bring to completion everything he had shown me through his holy Church. I asked him to strengthen me and to give repentance to his servants who had stumbled, so that his great and glorious Name might be honored. I was still thanking him for allowing me to see such things when I heard what sounded like a voice.

“Do not be double-minded, Hermas.”

That caught me off guard.

I began arguing with myself almost immediately.

*How can I be double-minded? The Lord has established me. I've seen extraordinary things. Surely by now I'm standing firm.*

I kept walking.

Then I noticed dust rising in the distance.

At first I assumed a herd of cattle must be coming toward me, kicking up dust as they moved. The cloud was perhaps a short distance away, but it kept growing.

And growing.

Soon I knew this wasn't cattle.

The sun broke through the dust just enough for me to see what was coming.

It was enormous.

A beast unlike anything I had ever seen was rushing toward me, something like a monstrous creature from the sea. It stretched nearly a hundred feet in length, and its head looked strangely hard, almost as though it were made of pottery. From its mouth poured fiery locusts.

I began to cry.

I begged the Lord to rescue me.

Then the words I had just heard returned to me.

*Do not be double-minded, Hermas.*

So I held onto my faith in the Lord. I remembered the mighty things he had already shown me, and somehow courage returned.

I stopped trying to escape.

Instead, I walked toward the beast.

It was charging with such force that it looked capable of destroying an entire city. But as I approached, the massive creature lowered itself to the ground.

It stretched out before me.

It put out its tongue.

And that was all.

It did not move again until I had walked completely past it.

Only then did I notice the colors upon its head.

There were four.

Black.

Then the color of fire and blood.

Then gold.

Then white.

I continued on.

After I'd gone only a short distance, I saw someone coming toward me.

She was a young woman dressed entirely in white, like a bride leaving her bridal chamber. She wore white sandals, a veil reaching to her forehead, and a turban over her head. Her hair was white.

I recognized her immediately.

It was the Church.

Just seeing her made me feel lighter.

“Good morning, my good man,” she said.

“Good morning, lady.”

She looked at me.

“Did anything meet you on the road?”

I almost laughed at the question.

“Lady, I met a beast so enormous it could have destroyed whole peoples. But through the Lord's power and his great mercy, I escaped it.”

“You escaped well,” she said.

Then she told me why.

“You placed your care upon God. You opened your heart to the Lord and believed that nothing could save you except his great and glorious Name.”

I listened carefully.

“So the Lord sent the angel who rules over the beasts. His name is Segri. He closed the beast's mouth so that it could not hurt you.”

Suddenly the creature lying silently in the road made sense.

“You escaped a great tribulation because of your faith. You saw something enormous and terrifying coming toward you, and still you did not give yourself over to doubt.”

Then she gave me a message to carry.

“Go and tell the Lord's chosen people about his mighty works. Tell them that the beast represents the great tribulation that is coming.”

Her warning became more urgent.

“If you prepare yourselves beforehand, repent, and turn to the Lord with your whole heart, you will be able to escape it. But your hearts must become pure and without blemish, and for the rest of your lives you must serve the Lord blamelessly.”

Then she said something simple enough to remember.

“Cast your cares upon the Lord. He will set them right.”

She spoke directly to everyone divided within themselves.

“Trust the Lord, you who are double-minded. He can do all things. He can turn his anger away from you, but he can also send judgment upon those who remain divided in heart.”

Her final words were severe.

“Woe to those who hear these words and refuse to obey them. It would have been better for them never to have been born.”

I had listened.

But of course I still had a question.

“Lady, what about the four colors on the beast's head?”

She gave me that familiar look.

“You're curious about these things again.”

“Yes, lady. Tell me what they mean.”

“Listen.”

She began with the black.

“The black is this world in which you live.”

Then the second color.

“The fire and blood show that this world must perish through blood and fire.”

Then the gold.

“The golden part represents those of you who have escaped from this world. Gold is tested in fire before it becomes useful. In the same way, you are being tested.”

She told me not to fear the testing itself.

“Those who remain faithful and pass through the fire will be purified by it. Just as gold loses its impurities, you will cast away sorrow and tribulation and become pure.”

Then she connected it to what I had already seen.

“You will become useful for the building of the tower.”

Finally, she explained the white.

“The white portion is the age that is coming. God's chosen people will live there, spotless and pure, into eternal life.”

Then she turned the whole vision into a warning.

“Do not stop speaking these things to the saints. Now you understand the sign of the tribulation that is coming with power.”

She paused.

“But if you are willing, it can become nothing to you.”

Then she told me to remember what had already been written.

With that, she left.

I couldn't tell where she went.

Suddenly I heard a loud noise behind me.

I turned around in fear.

For one terrible moment, I thought the beast was coming back.
