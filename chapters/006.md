## Vision 3d — The Woman Who Grew Young

The question stayed with me.

The first time I'd seen the woman, a year earlier, she had been extremely old and seated on a chair.

The second time, something had changed. Her face looked younger, but her body and hair were still old. She stood while speaking to me, and she seemed much happier than before.

But in this third vision she had appeared almost entirely young. She was extraordinarily beautiful. Only her hair remained old, and she was more joyful than I'd ever seen her. This time she sat on a couch.

I wanted desperately to understand why.

That night the old woman appeared to me again in a vision.

“Every question requires humility,” she said. “Fast, and you'll receive from the Lord what you're asking.”

So I fasted for one day.

That very night a young man appeared to me.

“You keep demanding revelations immediately,” he said. “Be careful. If you keep this up, all your asking may harm your body.”

Apparently even heaven had begun to think I asked too many questions.

“You've been shown enough,” he continued. “Do you think you can see things greater than what you've already seen?”

“Sir, I only want one thing.”

He waited.

“I want to understand the three forms of the old woman. That's all. If I understand that, the revelation will feel complete.”

He looked at me.

“How long will you remain without understanding? You're confused because you're divided within yourselves, because your hearts are not fully fixed on the Lord.”

I answered him as plainly as I could.

“Then teach me, sir. If you explain it, we'll understand it more clearly.”

“Listen, then.”

He began with the first vision.

“Why did she appear to you as an old woman, sitting on a chair? Because your spirit had grown old. It was already decaying and had become weak through your failures and your divided hearts.

“Think of elderly people who no longer expect to recover their youth. They have little left to anticipate except sleep and death. In the same way, you had been weakened by the affairs of this world. You surrendered yourselves to complaining instead of placing your cares upon the Lord.

“Your spirits were broken.

“Your sorrows had made you old.”

I thought about the chair.

“Then why was she sitting?”

“Because someone who is weak sits down so that the chair can support the weakness of the body. That is the meaning of what you saw in the first vision.”

Then he explained the second.

“In the second vision you saw her standing. Her face was younger and happier, though her body and hair remained old.”

He told me to imagine an elderly man who had lost all hope. He was weak and poor and expected nothing except the final day of his life.

“Then suddenly,” the young man said, “he learns that he has received an inheritance.”

Everything changes.

The old man rises.

Joy gives him strength. He no longer lies helplessly waiting for death. His broken spirit is renewed, and he stands again with courage.

“That is what happened to you when you heard what the Lord revealed.”

The Lord had shown compassion.

Our spirits had begun to renew. We laid aside our weakness. Strength returned. Faith made us powerful again, and the Lord rejoiced to see us recover our strength.

“That is why he showed you the tower being built,” the young man said. “And he will show you other things as well, if you live wholeheartedly at peace with one another.”

Then he came to the third vision.

“This time you saw the woman young, beautiful, and full of joy.”

He asked me to imagine someone consumed by grief who suddenly receives wonderful news.

“In that moment the person forgets the sorrow that came before. Nothing fills the mind except the good news. Strength returns. The spirit is renewed because joy has entered it.

“That is what has happened to you. Your spirits have been renewed by seeing these good things.”

Then he explained the couch.

“You saw her seated upon a couch because the couch represents firmness. It has four legs and stands securely, just as the world itself is upheld by four elements.”

The image became clear.

“Those who completely repent will become young again. They will be established firmly because they repented with their whole hearts.”

He looked at me as though he wanted to make sure I understood what was coming next.

“There. Now you have the whole revelation. It is complete.”

And then came the instruction I had heard many times already.

“Don't ask for any more revelations about this.”

He paused.

“But if something is still missing, it will be revealed to you.”
