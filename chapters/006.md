## 6 — While the Tower Is Still Unfinished

“I want to know, lady,” I said, “what power each of these women possesses.”

“Then listen.”

She told me that their powers followed one another according to their birth. From Faith came Continence; from Continence, Simplicity; from Simplicity, Guilelessness; from Guilelessness, Reverence; from Reverence, Knowledge; and from Knowledge, Love. Each led into the next, and their works were pure, reverent, and divine.

“Whoever serves these women and has strength to master their works will dwell in the tower with the saints of God.”

My curiosity immediately carried me further. I asked about the times and seasons. Was the end already near?

She cried out, “Foolish man! Can you not see that the tower is still being built? When the tower is finished, then the end will come. But it will be completed quickly. Ask me no more questions about this. This reminder is enough for you and the saints, for the renewal of your spirits.”

Then she reminded me that none of this had been shown for my sake alone. I was to understand her words and then speak them into the ears of the saints. They were to hear and do them, so that they might be purified from their wickedness.

And I was to be purified with them.

Then she spoke as a mother to her children.

“Hear me, my children. I raised you in simplicity, guilelessness, and reverence through the mercy of the Lord. He planted righteousness within you so that you might be made righteous and holy, free from wickedness and crookedness. Yet you refuse to cease from your evil.”

She called us to peace—to care for one another and help one another. Those who possessed God’s created gifts in abundance were not to consume them alone but to share with those who lacked what they needed.

Some injured their bodies through excessive eating, while others suffered because they had nothing to eat. Their bodies wasted away for lack of nourishment. There was something deeply wrong in an abundance that refused to share itself.

“Look toward the judgment that is coming. You who have more than enough, seek out those who are hungry while the tower remains unfinished. Once the tower is complete, you may desire to do good and discover there is no longer a place for it.”

Those who rejoiced in their wealth were to beware. The cries of the needy could rise before the Lord while those surrounded by abundance found themselves shut outside the tower.

Then her words turned toward the rulers of the Church, those who occupied the chief seats.

“Do not be like sorcerers. Sorcerers carry their drugs in boxes, but you carry your drug and poison within your hearts.”

Their hearts had grown hard. They possessed wisdom, yet would not cleanse themselves so that their wisdom might dwell within a pure heart. Their divisions threatened their very lives.

“How can you desire to instruct the Lord’s elect when you yourselves have not been instructed? Teach one another. Be at peace with one another, so that I may stand joyfully before the Father and give an account of you all to your Lord.”

When she finished, the six young men who had been building came and carried her toward the tower. Four others lifted the couch and carried it there as well, their faces turned away from me.

As she departed, one more question pressed upon me.

“Lady, why have I seen you in three different forms?”

But she would not answer.

“You must ask another if you want these things revealed.”

I could not stop thinking about it. In my first vision she had been extremely old, seated upon a chair. In the second, her face had become young while her flesh and hair remained aged, and she had stood before me more joyful than before. In the third, she had appeared altogether young and extraordinarily beautiful, except that her hair remained aged. She had been filled with joy and seated upon the couch.

I desperately wanted to understand.

That night the aged woman appeared to me again.

“Every inquiry requires humility,” she said. “Fast, therefore, and you will receive from the Lord what you ask.”
