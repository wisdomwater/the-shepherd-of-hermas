## Parable 9e — Clothed for the Tower

“And the tower?” I asked.

“The tower is the Church.”

“And the virgins?”

“They are holy spirits.”

He told me that no one could be found in the kingdom of God unless these virgins clothed him with their garments.

Simply carrying the name wasn't enough.

“If you receive the name but not their clothing, it profits you nothing. These virgins are powers of the Son of God. If you carry his name without carrying his power, you carry the name uselessly.”

“What is their clothing?”

“Their names.”

Then he named them.

The four strongest—the ones I had seen standing at the corners—were Faith, Continence, Power, and Long-suffering.

The others were Simplicity, Guilelessness, Purity, Cheerfulness, Truth, Understanding, Concord, and Love.

Whoever bore the name of the Son of God was also to clothe himself in these.

That was what I had seen happening to the stones.

When the virgins received them and carried them through the gate, the stones were clothed in their power. Their differences disappeared. They became one color and fit together so completely that the tower seemed to be a single stone joined to the great rock beneath it.

“So it is with those who believe in the Lord through his Son,” the shepherd said. “When they clothe themselves in these spirits, they become one spirit and one body.”

But some stones had been removed even after the virgins had carried them through the gate.

“Why?”

“Because they received the name of the Son of God and the power of the virgins. They were strengthened. They lived among the servants of God with one spirit, one body, one mind, and they practiced righteousness.”

Then they encountered the women dressed in black.

They saw their beauty and desired them.

They clothed themselves with the power of those women and stripped away the power of the virgins.

That was why they had been thrown from the house of God and handed over to the women whose power they had chosen.

I immediately wanted to know whether that decision could be reversed.

“What if they repent? What if they abandon their desire for these women, return to the virgins, and begin living again in their power? Can they enter the house of God?”

“They can.”

That was why the building had paused.

Time remained for repentance.

If they abandoned the works of the women in black and clothed themselves again with the power of the virgins, they could return to the tower.

If they refused, others would take their places.

Hearing that, I thanked the Lord.

We had sinned against him, and some of us had nearly destroyed ourselves. Yet he had sent the angel of repentance to revive our spirits when we had almost lost hope of life.

Then I asked why the tower had been built upon the rock and the gate rather than simply upon the ground.

“Because you're senseless and without understanding.”

“I have to ask, Sir. I can't understand these things on my own. They're too great for me.”

“The name of the Son of God is great beyond understanding,” he said, “and it sustains the whole world.”

If the Son sustained creation itself, then surely he could sustain those who carried his name wholeheartedly and walked in his commandments.

“He has become their foundation,” the shepherd told me. “And he gladly sustains them because they are not ashamed to bear his name.”

Then I asked about the women in black.

Their names stood opposite the names of the virgins.

The four strongest were Unbelief, Intemperance, Disobedience, and Deceit.

Following them came Sadness, Wickedness, Wantonness, Irascibility, Falsehood, Folly, Slander, and Hatred.

The servant of God who clothed himself in these might see the kingdom.

But he would not enter it.
