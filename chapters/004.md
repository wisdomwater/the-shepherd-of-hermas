## 4 — The Stones of the Tower

I watched the stones being brought up from the deep. Each one was placed into the tower exactly as it was, for they had already been shaped. They fitted so perfectly with the stones beside them that no seam could be seen between one and another. The whole tower seemed not to be made from many stones at all, but from a single stone.

The stones brought from the dry land were different. Some were accepted into the building, while others were thrown aside. Some were broken into pieces and cast far away from the tower.

Still more stones lay scattered around it, unused. Some were mildewed. Some were cracked, others too short, while still others were white and round, unable to fit into the building.

I saw stones that had been thrown far from the tower roll toward the road, only to pass beyond it into places where there was no road. Others fell into fire and burned. Still others came near the waters and seemed to desire to roll into them, yet could not reach the water.

After showing me all this, the aged woman prepared to leave.

“Lady,” I said, “what good does it do me to see these things if I do not know what they mean?”

“You are an over-curious fellow,” she answered, “always wanting to know everything about the tower.”

“Yes, lady, because I want to tell my brothers and sisters. When they hear these things, they may know the Lord in his great glory.”

“Many will hear,” she said. “Some will rejoice, and others will weep. But even those who weep will rejoice if they hear and repent. So listen to the parables of the tower, and I will reveal everything to you. And stop troubling me about revelations. They have reached their end. Still, I know you will not stop asking. You are shameless.”

Then she told me plainly, “The tower you see being built is myself—the Church whom you have seen before. Ask whatever you wish about the tower, and I will reveal it so that you may rejoice with the saints.”

“Then, lady, since you have considered me worthy to receive these things, reveal them to me.”

“Whatever can be revealed to you will be revealed. Only keep your heart with God, and do not doubt what you see.”

I asked first why the tower was being built upon the waters.

“You were told this before,” she said, “but you inquire diligently, and through your questions you discover the truth. The tower stands upon the waters because your life is saved and shall be saved through water. Yet its foundation is the word of the Almighty and Glorious Name, and it is strengthened by the unseen power of the Master.”

“This is great and marvelous,” I said. “But who are the six young men building the tower?”

“They are the holy angels of God, created first of all. The Lord entrusted his creation to them so that they might increase it, build it, and govern it. Through their hands the tower will be completed.”

“And the others carrying the stones?”

“They too are holy angels, though the six are greater. When the tower is completed, all of them together will rejoice and glorify God because the building has been finished.”

I wanted to understand the stones themselves.

She reminded me that I had not received these visions because I was better or more worthy than others. There were others before me who were better than I was. These things had been shown so that God’s name might be glorified and so that those who doubted in their hearts might know that the visions were true, firm, and established upon a sure foundation.

Then she began to explain.

“The square white stones whose joints fit perfectly together are apostles, bishops, teachers, and deacons. They walked in God’s holiness and served his elect in purity. Some have fallen asleep and some still live. They lived in agreement, at peace with one another, listening to one another. That is why their joints fit together so perfectly.”

“And those drawn from the deep?”

“They are those who suffered for the name of the Lord.”

“What about the stones from the dry land that enter the building without being cut?”

“The Lord has approved them. They walked uprightly before him and rightly kept his commandments.”

“And those brought and placed into the tower?”

“They are young in the faith, yet faithful. The angels warn them to do good, because wickedness has still been found in them.”
