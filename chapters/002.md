# Vision 2 — The Woman and the Little Book

A year had passed, and once again I was traveling toward Cumae. As I walked, I found myself thinking about everything that had happened there the year before—the strange country, the opened heavens, the old woman and her book. Then, just as before, a Spirit took me and carried me away.

I found myself in the same place.

I fell to my knees and prayed. This time I thanked the Lord and praised his name because he had considered me worthy to see what I'd seen and to recognize the sins I hadn't recognized before. When I finished praying and stood, there she was again: the elderly woman from the previous vision, walking toward me while reading from a small book.

“Can you report these things to God's chosen people?” she asked.

“Lady, I can't remember that much.”

She waited.

“Give me the book,” I said. “I'll copy it.”

“Take it. But make sure you return it to me.”

I took the little book and found a quiet place in the countryside. Then I began copying it, letter by letter. That was the strange thing: I could copy the individual letters, but I couldn't make sense of the syllables or understand what I was writing. I simply reproduced what I saw.

The moment I finished, the book was suddenly torn from my hand.

I never saw who took it.

For the next fifteen days I fasted and earnestly asked the Lord to help me. Then, at last, the meaning of what I had copied was opened to me.

The message began with my own family.

“Your children, Hermas, have sinned against God. They have blasphemed the Lord and betrayed their parents through great wickedness. They have even earned the name ‘betrayers of parents,’ though their betrayal gained them nothing. And they continued adding sin to sin—recklessness, corruption, and wickedness—until their wrongdoing reached its full measure.

“Tell these things to all your children. Tell them also to your wife, who is to be as your sister. She has not controlled her tongue, and with it she does evil. But when she hears these words, she will restrain herself, and she will receive mercy.”

There was mercy in the message, but it came with an urgency I couldn't ignore.

“When you have told them everything the Master commanded me to reveal to you, the sins they committed before this will be forgiven. The same is true for all the saints who have sinned up to this day, if they repent with their whole hearts and drive double-mindedness from within them.

“For the Master has sworn by his own glory concerning his chosen ones: a day has been established as a boundary. If they continue in sin beyond it, they will not find salvation. Repentance for the righteous has an end. The days of repentance for the saints are reaching their completion, though repentance remains open to the Gentiles until the last day.

“So tell the elders of the Church to make their paths straight in righteousness, so that they may receive everything that has been promised to them, with abundant glory.

“And you who practice righteousness, stand firm. Don't be divided within yourselves. Then you will be welcomed among the holy angels. Blessed are those who patiently endure the great tribulation that is coming and do not deny their life.

“For the Lord has sworn concerning his Son that those who deny their Lord will be rejected from their life. This concerns those who are preparing to deny him in the days ahead. But those who denied him before this were shown mercy because of his great compassion.”

Then the words turned toward me again.

“Hermas, stop holding your children's sins against them. And don't simply let your sister do whatever she pleases. Give them the opportunity to be cleansed from what they have done. They will receive righteous correction, but you must not carry resentment against them. Resentment produces death.”

That struck close.

“You've suffered greatly because of your family's sins, but you also neglected them. You didn't give them the attention they needed because you became entangled in your own bad affairs.”

There was no easy way around that accusation.

“But your salvation is found in this: you did not abandon the living God. Your simplicity and your self-control have preserved you. Remain in them. These things save everyone who walks without deceit and with a simple heart. Such people overcome wickedness and continue into eternal life.

“Blessed are all who practice righteousness. They will never be destroyed.”

There was one more message.

“Tell Maximus this: ‘Look, tribulation is coming upon you if you intend to deny again. The Lord is near to those who turn back to him.’ This is written in Eldad and Modat, who prophesied to the people in the wilderness.”

Later, while I slept, another revelation came to me.

A young man appeared, strikingly beautiful.

“Who do you think the elderly woman was who gave you the book?”

“The Sibyl,” I said.

“You're wrong.”

That surprised me. “Then who is she?”

“The Church.”

I couldn't understand that either.

“Then why is she so old?”

“Because she was created before everything else. That is why she appears old. The world itself was formed for her sake.”

Afterward, while I was at home, I saw the old woman again.

“Have you given the book to the elders yet?” she asked.

“No.”

“You've done well. I still have words to add. When I've finished everything, you will make it all known to God's chosen people.”

Then she told me how it was to be done.

“You will make two copies. Send one to Clement and one to Grapte. Clement will send his copy to the cities abroad, because that responsibility belongs to him. Grapte will instruct the widows and the orphans.

“But you, Hermas, will read the book here in this city, together with the elders who preside over the Church.”
