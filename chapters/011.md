## Mandate 3 — Let Nothing but Truth Come From You

“Love the truth,” the shepherd told me. “Let nothing but truth come from your mouth.”

The Spirit God had placed within this body, he said, must be found truthful before everyone.

“Then the Lord who lives within you will be glorified, because the Lord is true in everything he says. There is no falsehood in him.”

But a person who lies does something more serious than simply deceive another human being.

“Those who lie treat the Lord with contempt,” he said. “They become robbers of God because they do not return to him what he entrusted to them.”

God had given us a spirit free from falsehood.

“If you return that spirit to him corrupted by lies, you have defiled what the Lord entrusted to you.”

When I heard this, I started crying.

The shepherd looked at me.

“Why are you crying?”

“Because, sir, I don't know whether I can be saved.”

“Why wouldn't you be?”

I could barely say it.

“Because I don't think I've ever spoken truthfully in my life. I've lied to people constantly. I've been deceitful, and I've dressed my lies so carefully that everyone believed they were true. No one challenged me. People trusted what I said.”

The weight of that realization had suddenly become unbearable.

“So how can I live, sir, after doing all of that?”

He didn't tell me I was exaggerating.

“What you're thinking is right,” he said. “As a servant of God, you should have walked in truth. Nothing evil should have been mixed with the Spirit of truth. You should never have brought grief to the holy and truthful Spirit.”

“Sir, I've never heard this explained so clearly before.”

“Well, now you have.”

There was no reason left for ignorance.

“Guard these words. From this point forward, speak only the truth. If you do that, even the things you said before in your business dealings can come to be regarded differently because your life will now be found truthful.”

Then he gave me hope.

“If you keep this command and from now on refuse falsehood, you can gain life for yourself.

“And everyone who hears this commandment and turns away from lying—that destructive habit—will live to God.”
