## Vision 3c — While the Tower Is Still Being Built

When I finally stopped questioning her about the stones, the woman looked at me.

“Would you like to see something else?”

I was delighted.

By now she knew there was no need to ask.

She smiled.

“Do you see the seven women standing around the tower?”

“I see them, lady.”

“The Lord has commanded that the tower be supported by them. Listen, and I'll tell you who they are.”

She pointed to the first woman, whose hands looked strong.

“She is Faith. Through her God's chosen people are saved.”

The second woman was dressed for action and had something almost masculine in her bearing.

“She is Continence, the daughter of Faith. Anyone who follows her becomes blessed in life, because that person turns away from evil deeds, trusting that by refusing evil desires he will inherit eternal life.”

“And the others?”

“They come from one another, each one the daughter of the one before her. They are Simplicity, Guilelessness, Reverence, Knowledge, and Love. If you practice the works of their mother, you will live.”

I wanted to understand more.

“What power does each one have?”

“Listen carefully. Their powers follow one another in the same order in which they were born. From Faith comes Continence. From Continence comes Simplicity. From Simplicity comes Guilelessness. From Guilelessness comes Reverence. From Reverence comes Knowledge. From Knowledge comes Love.”

She looked at the seven women surrounding the tower.

“Their works are pure, reverent, and divine. Anyone who serves these women and becomes strong in their works will have a home within the tower among the saints of God.”

Naturally, another question immediately came to me.

“Lady, what about the times? Is the end already near?”

She nearly shouted at me.

“Foolish man! Can't you see that the tower is still being built?”

I fell quiet.

“When the tower is finished, then the end will come. But the tower will be completed quickly. Don't ask me any more questions about this. What you've already been shown is enough to remind you and all the saints and to renew your spirits.”

Then she made it clear that none of this had been given for me alone.

“You were shown these things so that you could show them to everyone else. But first you need to understand them yourself.”

She gave me three days.

Then she told me exactly what I was to say to the saints.

“My children, listen to me. Through the Lord's mercy I raised you in simplicity, guilelessness, and reverence. He planted righteousness within you so that you might be made righteous and holy, cleansed from wickedness and crookedness.”

Her voice grew heavy.

“But you still refuse to stop doing evil.

“So listen now. Live at peace with one another. Pay attention to each other. Help one another. Do not enjoy God's creation in abundance while keeping it all for yourselves. Share what you have with those who are in need.”

She gave an example that was impossible to misunderstand.

“Some people eat so much that they weaken their own bodies. Others have so little food that their bodies are destroyed from lack of nourishment. Both are being harmed—and the reason is that those who have enough refuse to share with those who don't.”

Then came the warning.

“Remember the judgment that is coming.

“If you have more than you need, go and find those who are hungry while the tower is still unfinished. Once the tower is complete, you may want to do good and discover that there is no longer any place for it.”

Her words turned directly toward those who were wealthy.

“You who glory in your riches, be careful. The needy may cry out because of what they lack, and their cries may rise before the Lord. Then you, surrounded by abundance, may find yourselves shut outside the tower.”

Then she spoke to the leaders of the Church.

“And you who rule the Church and sit in the highest places, don't be like people who hide poison in little boxes. They carry their drugs in containers. You carry yours inside your hearts.”

The accusation was sharp.

“Your hearts have become hardened. You refuse to cleanse them. You refuse to bring your wisdom together within a pure heart so that you might receive mercy from the Great King.

“My children, be careful that your divisions do not cost you your life.”

Then she asked them a question that left nowhere to hide.

“How can you expect to teach the Lord's chosen people when you yourselves refuse to be taught?”

She told them to begin with one another.

“Teach one another. Make peace with one another. Then I may stand joyfully before the Father and give an account of all of you to your Lord.”

When she finished speaking, the six young men who had been building the tower returned.

They took her with them toward the tower.

Four others lifted the couch and carried it away as well. Their faces were turned from me, so I never saw them.

As the woman was leaving, I remembered one last thing.

“Lady, wait! I want to know about the different ways you've appeared to me.”

She looked back.

“You'll have to ask someone else about that.”

And then she was gone.
